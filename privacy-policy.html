Privacy Policy

InternNova
Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Welcome to InternNova. InternNova is an internship management platform designed to connect students with mentors and companies and to simplify the internship management process.

This Privacy Policy explains how InternNova collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by users while using the InternNova application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use InternNova, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Educational information
  • Skills, interests, and career-related information
  • Resume or other documents uploaded by the user
  • Internship applications and related information
  • Internship tasks, reports, feedback, and submissions
  • Account login and authentication information
  • Information provided while communicating with mentors or companies

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected through InternNova may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide internship opportunities to students
  • Allow students to apply for internships
  • Allow mentors or companies to manage internships and applications
  • Assign and manage internship tasks
  • Allow students to submit internship reports
  • Provide feedback and evaluate internship progress
  • Provide administrative and reporting functionality
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of the application
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the platform

3. Information Sharing

InternNova does not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be shared within the platform when necessary to provide internship-related services. For example, information submitted by a student may be made available to relevant mentors or companies when the student applies for an internship.

Information may also be accessible to authorized administrators for legitimate platform management, security, and reporting purposes.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain user information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain accounts, fulfill legitimate operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. User Rights

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable requirements.

If you wish to request changes to your information or deletion of your account, please contact the InternNova team.

7. Children's Privacy

InternNova is intended for users who are eligible to participate in internships and related educational or professional activities. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

8. Third-Party Services

InternNova may use trusted third-party services for purposes such as authentication, database management, hosting, analytics, or other essential application functionality.

Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published through the appropriate InternNova platform or website.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact the InternNova team.
